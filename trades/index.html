Method

Data
CME Globex NQ one-minute candles (Databento GLBX.MDP3), front month, 2018 to Oct 23 2026. Aggressor-side order flow from Oct 2023. QQQ options open interest (OPRA) from Oct 2025.
Costs
1 tick of slippage on every market fill and $4.50 commission per NQ round trip, taken out of every trade.
R
Each trade risks 1R: the distance from entry to the first stop. Dollar figures are R times the amount set at the top of the page.
Sharpe and drawdown
Daily R including days without a trade, annualised with √252 and no risk-free rate. Drawdowns use end-of-day totals. VWAP counts only sessions with order flow; the wall break only sessions with options data.
Time
Central time (CME). Brackets: A is 8:30 to 9:00 CT, B is 9:00 to 9:30, through M at 14:30 to 15:00.
Source
The same trade logs as the research page.
